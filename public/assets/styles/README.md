Photos for the "Your style" cards in onboarding and the fit profile.

Add one JPG per style, named by its id, for example `casual.jpg` or `ethnic_chic.jpg`.
The ids are: casual, formal, traditional, indo_western, western, party, sportswear, ethnic_chic, minimalist, street, resort, retro.

Use only images Dori may use: your own photos, or photos with a licence that allows commercial use (e.g. Unsplash or Pexels).
Each card shows a Dori line illustration until its photo is added.
